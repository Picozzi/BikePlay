Build Log - Week 1

January 20, 2023

This has been an exciting first week for the building of BikePlay!

Most components have now arrived, with the exceptions of several wires and a GPIO terminal block, which are expected to arrive during the weekend.

In the mechanical department, all 3D prints for the projection system housing have been completed. This includes the housing and mounting adapter for the projection system for the left-side of the helmet (see below).

All parts were printed using PLA, due to its low-cost for prototyping and material availability. We are happy to report that all prints were successful and mate together properly!

Once the design is verified, components for the right-side projection system will be printed. This iterative approach for building was done for cost-efficiency purposes, since the right side is a mirror image of the left side.

In addition to this, the visor and visor adapter were successfully laser cut (see below)!

The visor was cut from a sheet of 1.5 mm clear acrylic to minimize the weight and the adapter was cut from a sheet of 4.5 mm clear acrylic to ensure it would be strong enough to support the visor.

On the software side of the project, the app is 60% complete, with the design of the main navigation page complete (see below).

We are currently testing the GPS navigation on the app but preliminary results are promising: tests while walking gave the correct directions with appropriate reaction time.
