Build Log - Week 4

February 10, 2023

We made significant progress on BikePlay this week!

First, all components for Prototype 2 were prepared: the lens was successfully cut and the new header pins were soldered onto the OLED. Prototype 2 was then fully assembled and tested. The lens was inserted into its slot, the OLED was wired up and mounted, and the projection system was mounted to the helmet.

During testing, the image was visible and clear! We found that the image was difficult to see when facing direct sunlight, however placing sunglasses in front of the visor made the image visible. So, we purchased a tint to apply on top of the visor and will test with it over the weekend.

Unfortunately, we experienced an electrical failure with the LiPo charging circuit, and after spending many hours troubleshooting, have been unable to determine the fault. So, we have decided to purchase an off-the-shelf charging circuit instead.

After testing last weekend, the app is successfully able to send directions to a Raspberry Pi. Due to component availability at the time of testing, a Raspberry Pi 3B+ was used instead of a Raspberry Pi Nano. The GATT server was able to run on the 3B+, so we are confident that it will be able to run on the Nano.

The app is mostly complete, with all remaining work being done to improve its appearance and the user-experience.

Finally, all team members attended the Ritual of the Calling of an Engineer and received their iron rings last weekend!
