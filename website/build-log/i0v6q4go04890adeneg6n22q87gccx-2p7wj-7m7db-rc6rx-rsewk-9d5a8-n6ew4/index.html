Build Log - Week 8

March 10, 2023

It’s cleanup week!

The new visors were laser cut and the polarized tinted sheets have been applied to them. This means that users could now wear the helmet in various lighting conditions and be able to properly read all visuals and see their surroundings.

The only outstanding firmware task, to create all the weather icons, was also completed last weekend. BikePlay now has icons for sunny, rainy, cloudy, foggy, snow, thunderstorm, and severe weather. Here’s a shot of the OLED for the right side, with the cloudy icon visible in the top right corner.

The code clean-up for software, including the app and data transfer between the app and the RPi, is also complete.

With all systems complete, a lot of testing was done last weekend. All group members drove around the city, with passengers wearing and testing BikePlay. This was helpful to find and correct issues and edge cases in the program. Testing like this will continue over the next couple weeks, until the symposium. Here’s a shot of the inside of the helmet.

BikePlay’s new logo is still in development and a marketing video is also now in the works - stay tuned!
