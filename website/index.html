The smart
bike helmet

Our goal is to create a product that places commonly used phone features, such as navigation, in front of a cyclists’ eyes using augmented reality.

Explore the specs Read the build log

Using your phone while biking isn’t safe - we want to change that.

Visor projections in a non-intrusive way

Our product leverages the bulk of a helmet to mount AR technology. This AR technology then displays information onto the sides of the helmet’s visor. The projection location is completely customizable to fit your needs!

Display the most meaningful information

Our product aims to provide 5 pieces of key information:

  • Navigation Directions

  • Time

  • Weather

  • Distance Travelled

  • Helmet Battery

Manage your helmet from your smartphone

(but not while you are riding)

Control Bluetooth connections, search for bike routes, visualize battery levels, and more.

Did we mention that cellular connection is not required to use most features of BikePlay?
